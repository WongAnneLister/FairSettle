# F35 · Token budget for reasoning models

## Goal

Behavior (copied from docs/features.json):

> Model calls leave room for a reasoning model's hidden reasoning (cap 6000 tokens, 90 s timeout), and an investigate turn cut off by the cap ends as truncated instead of as done.

Verification (copied from docs/features.json, `kind: command`):

```bash
cd agent && python -m pytest -q tests/test_token_budget.py
```

In F27's evaluation, scenario s05 ("Already refunded") was correct only once in three runs. The two misses were `decide/decision_invalid`.

### What the probe found (2026-11-02)

A read-only probe re-ran s05 against `deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro`, recording every raw reply. The scripts and logs are only in the planning session's scratchpad, not in the repository.

- **With today's cap.** In three runs, most investigate and decide calls ended with `finish_reason="length"` at exactly 1200 completion tokens, and the content was empty or a half-written JSON. Two runs escalated as `decision_invalid: truncated` after `decide`'s single retry was also cut off. One run passed on the retry, which used 528 tokens.
- **With a high cap.** Three single `decide` calls with `max_tokens=8000` finished normally (`finish_reason="stop"`):

  | Call | Reasoning tokens | Completion tokens | Visible JSON | Time |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | 2973 | 3094 | 432 chars | 43.6 s |
  | 2 | 2162 | 2266 | 422 chars | 32.0 s |
  | 3 | 1644 | 1757 | 379 chars | 25.4 s |

  Earlier calls: reasoning 685 and 1604. The responses carry `reasoning_content`.
- **Why.** `deepseek-v4-pro` writes hidden reasoning that counts against `max_tokens`. With `ModelConfig.max_tokens=1200`, a case that needs thought runs out before the JSON. The 30-second `timeout_s` is also below the 44 s observed.
- **A second defect.** `investigate` treats a reply cut off by the cap like a reply without tool calls: `stopped="done"`, silently. This happened in the probe: run 1's and run 3's investigate turns were truncated, and nothing recorded it.

### Decisions (user, 2026-11-02)

1. **New defaults.** `ModelConfig.max_tokens = 6000` and `timeout_s = 90.0`, about twice the largest measurement. `max_tokens` is a cap; only used tokens are billed. The existing validation (1–8000) stays.
2. **The M2 live-run script waits 600 s** for `POST /v1/complaints`, instead of 180 s. One complaint may make up to 3 investigate and 2 decide calls.

Planner decisions:
- **A truncated investigate turn ends the investigation** with `stopped="truncated"`. Any tool calls in that reply are not executed, because they may be incomplete. Findings from earlier steps are kept, and `decide` runs as usual.
- **`decide` keeps its retry on `truncated`.** Only its budget changes.

### What was checked (2026-11-02)

## Out of scope

- Prompt changes; turning reasoning off; per-stage budgets. One budget serves `investigate` and `decide`.
- The F28 UI's handling of long waits. `POST /v1/complaints` stays synchronous.
- Rerunning the evaluation as part of `verify`. Step 10 does it by hand.

## Risks

- **Longer worst-case waits.** Up to 5 calls of up to 90 s each per complaint. In the probe, a typical call took 25–45 s.
- **6000 may still be short for some future case.** It would then show as `truncated` in the investigation metadata, or as `decision_invalid: truncated`. Both are visible, so it no longer passes silently.
